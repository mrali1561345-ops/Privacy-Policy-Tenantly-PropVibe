# Privacy Policy for Tenantly PropVibe

**Effective Date:** November 02, 2026

Tenantly PropVibe ("we", "our", or "app") is built as a free, lightweight utility tool to help property owners manage rental properties and tenant payments. This Privacy Policy outlines how your information is handled when you use our mobile application.

### 1. Data Collection & Storage
- Tenantly PropVibe operates entirely **locally** on your device. 
- We **do not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personal tenant records, phone numbers, or rental amounts on external servers or cloud databases. All your entered data stays securely on your device.

### 2. Permissions
- The app does not require sensitive device permissions (such as camera, microphone, or precise location) to function. 

### 3. Third-Party Services
- Since our app does not integrate external tracking SDKs or ad networks, your usage data remains completely private.

### 4. Changes to This Privacy Policy
- We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
